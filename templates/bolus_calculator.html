Algorithm

Documented in Annex II as a software component.

bolus = carb_dose + correction_dose + trend_adjustment − IOB carb_dose = carbohydrates ÷ insulin_to_carb_ratio correction_dose = (glucose − target) ÷ insulin_sensitivity_factor trend_adjustment = {rising_fast:+1.5, rising:+0.75, steady:0, falling:−0.75, falling_fast:−1.5} result is clamped at ≥ 0 U

Risk controls

  • • Insulin on board is subtracted to reduce dose stacking
  • • Hypoglycaemia and ketone thresholds raise explicit warnings
  • • Falling trends at low glucose block a silent correction dose
  • • Output is decision support only — no insulin delivery

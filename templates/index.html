Welcome to APProved

Unlocking compliance as growth

APProved is a two-view medical writing platform that pairs agentic AI with human expert review.

  • Client View: Upload your clinical data, specify requirements (tone, audience, regulations, target markets), and review generated deliverables.
  • User View: As an APProved expert, compose prompts per deliverable type, inherit client requirements, refine outputs, and manage multi-round reviews.
